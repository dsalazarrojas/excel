What it does

Five reconciliation steps

Exact, folio, text similarity, assisted review and group matching. Every pair shows its method, confidence and a note.

One deposit against several invoices

Finds one entry equal to the sum of several on the other side. It only reconciles automatically when they share a folio; everything else is a suggestion.

You decide

Every suggestion has ✓ and ✕ buttons. What you accept is reconciled; what you reject goes back to pending.

Marks your own sheets

Writes a “Reconciliation status” column next to your data and colors the rows: green, amber or red. It overwrites nothing and one button removes it.

Assisted review only when needed

Ambiguous pairs can go through a licensed assisted review. Without a license everything else keeps working.

Results in a new sheet

A report with dates, amounts, method and note for each pair, plus the entries still pending on each side.

How it works

  1. 1

    Select your data

    Select the ledger range and the bank statement range. The add-in proposes the date, description and amount columns; it supports separate debit/credit columns or a signed amount.

  2. 2

    Reconcile

    The comparison runs on your computer. You see reconciled pairs, suggestions and pending entries.

  3. 3

    Review and mark

    Accept or reject suggestions, mark the result in your sheets or create the results sheet.

Your data

  • The standard comparison runs on your computer, inside Excel.
  • Only ambiguous pairs (date, type, amount and description of two entries) are sent to the assisted review, and only with a license. Your full ledger is never sent.
  • Our service processes those pairs in memory and does not store them.

Requirements and installation

  • Microsoft 365 Excel, Excel 2019 or later (Windows and Mac), or Excel on the web. Tested on Excel for Mac (Office 2024).
  • Marking your sheets needs a recent Excel version; on older versions everything else works.
Excel on the web

Insert → Add-ins → Upload My Add-in and choose the manifest.xml we send you.

Excel for Mac

Copy manifest.xml to ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Excel/Data/Documents/wef (create the folder if needed), restart Excel and open the Conciliar button on the Home tab.

Excel for Windows

Put manifest.xml in a shared folder, add it under File → Options → Trust Center → Trusted Add-in Catalogs, restart Excel and open it from Insert → My Add-ins → Shared Folder.

FAQ

What format should my data have?

A table with headers in the first row: date, description and amount (or debit and credit columns). Up to 20,000 rows per range.

My bank statement has one signed amount column

That works. Choose “Use the sign of the amount” and negatives are treated as debits.

Does it change my sheets?

Only if you press “Mark the result in my sheets”. It writes one column to the right of your range (it stops if that column is not empty) and a button removes the marks.

What if I do not have a license?

Everything works except the assisted review of ambiguous pairs, which stay unscored.
